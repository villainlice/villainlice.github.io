3. Cookies and analytics

On your first visit a cookie banner is shown. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity are not loaded, and their cookies (e.g. _ga, _clck, _clsk) are not set, unless you click "Accept". Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage (gtv_consent_v1), which is necessary for the site to remember it. We do not use advertising cookies.

You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" button in the corner of the page. If you withdraw a previous acceptance, the page reloads to stop loading optional analytics on subsequent page views. Withdrawing consent does not automatically delete data already collected by external providers.
